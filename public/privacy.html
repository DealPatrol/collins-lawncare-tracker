TurfRoute Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

TurfRoute ("the App") helps lawn-care businesses track crews, routes, time, mileage and customer jobs. This policy explains what the App stores, where, and how to delete it.

Summary

  • No ads, no advertising trackers, no analytics SDKs, and we never sell your data.
  • Without an account, everything stays on your device.
  • With an account, your business data syncs to your private cloud storage so it survives a lost phone.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time in Settings → Account → Delete Account.

Information the App stores

  • Account: your email address and an encrypted password, managed by Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Business data you enter: jobs, customer names, addresses, phone numbers and emails, pay rates, notes, crew names, prospects, expenses and settings. Stored on your device and, when you are signed in, in Google Cloud Firestore under your account only.
  • Location (while using the App): GPS coordinates when you start/stop a workday or job timer, job-site pins, geofence arrival/departure and mileage. Location is only collected while the App is open and only to power those features.
  • Purchases: TurfRoute Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple. We receive only whether your Apple ID has an active subscription; we never see your payment details.

Services that process data for the App

  • Google Firebase (authentication and cloud sync).
  • Stripe — when your customers pay an invoice through the customer portal, Stripe processes the card payment. Payment records are kept by Stripe as required for tax and accounting.
  • SendGrid — sends invoice and job-completion emails to customers when you choose to.
  • Open-Meteo — receives approximate coordinates to return the weather forecast. No account or identifier is sent.
  • Optional services you configure with your own API keys: Regrid (public parcel records near a location), Anthropic (AI yard-size estimate from a satellite image of a lead's location — only when you tap Estimate), and a webhook URL you provide for job-done texts. Data is sent to these only when you use those features.

Data retention and deletion

Data is kept until you delete it. Delete Account (Settings → Account) permanently deletes your Firebase account and your synced cloud data, and erases the copy on the device. Signing out also removes the local copy from that device. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; manage that in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Payment records processed by Stripe on behalf of your customers may be retained as required by law.

Children

The App is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: contact the developer through the support link on the App Store listing.
