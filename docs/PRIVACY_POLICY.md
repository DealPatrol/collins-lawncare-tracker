# Privacy Policy — Collins Lawncare Tracker

**Last updated:** July 2026

Collins Lawncare Tracker ("the App") is a mobile schedule and pay tracking tool for lawn care professionals.

## Summary

- The App stores all data **locally on your device**
- We do **not** collect, sell, or share your personal data with third parties
- The App does **not** require an account or login
- The App does **not** use analytics or advertising trackers

## Information the App uses

### Location data

The App may request access to your device's location **while you are using the App** to:

- Log GPS coordinates when you start a job timer
- Record your location when starting or ending a workday
- Save optional coordinates for job sites

Location data is stored only on your device. It is not transmitted to our servers because the App has no backend.

### Job and business data

You may enter customer names, addresses, pay rates, notes, and work session history. This information is stored locally on your device using browser storage (localStorage).

### Backup files

If you use Export Backup in Settings, a JSON file is saved to your device or cloud storage of your choice (e.g. Files app, iCloud). We do not receive these files.

## Data retention

Data remains on your device until you delete it, uninstall the App, or restore from a backup file.

## Children's privacy

The App is intended for business use and is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app owner at your business support email or website.
