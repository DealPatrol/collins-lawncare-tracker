Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

1. Introduction

Collins Lawncare Tracker ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application. The App is designed with privacy-first principles: all data is stored locally on your device.

2. Data Storage

Local Storage Only: The Collins Lawncare Tracker stores all user data locally on your device using browser/app localStorage. This includes:

  • Job names and details
  • Work session records and time tracking data
  • Crew name
  • Payment information
  • Photos and completion notes
  • Client contact information (optional)

No Cloud Backup: Your data is never transmitted to external servers or stored in the cloud unless you explicitly export it. We do not have access to your data.

3. Permissions

The App may request the following device permissions:

  • Camera: To capture photos of completed work (optional, user-initiated)
  • Location: To track GPS coordinates for job locations and route optimization (optional)
  • Storage: To read/write data locally on your device

All permissions are optional and only used when you explicitly enable them.

4. Data Security

Since your data remains on your device, its security depends on your device's operating system security measures. We recommend:

  • Keeping your device software updated
  • Using device-level authentication (passcode, biometric)
  • Regularly backing up your device
  • Exporting your data periodically for external backup

5. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with third-party services only when you explicitly use them:

  • Google Maps: Used for route mapping and location display (optional)
  • Device Camera: Used for capturing photos

These services have their own privacy policies. Please review them at their respective websites.

6. Data Export & Deletion

Your Data, Your Control: You can export all your data at any time through the App's settings. To delete all data:

  • Use the App's built-in reset feature, or
  • Clear the App's localStorage through device settings, or
  • Uninstall the App

7. Analytics & Crash Reporting

The App does not collect analytics, crash reports, or usage data. We do not track your behavior or activity.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete such information promptly.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you through the App or by updating the "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of the App after any modifications indicates your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at:

  • Email: support@collinslawncare.com

Collins Lawncare Tracker respects your privacy and keeps your data where it belongs: on your device, under your control.
